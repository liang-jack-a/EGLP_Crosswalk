The livable-area and population-based weights (livable_land.py, towns.py, crosswalk_county_population.py,
crosswalk_cz_population.py) read these files from this folder, in addition to the shapefiles in Shapefiles/:

    Inputs/gmted2010/*_gmted_mea075.tif                   (elevation, five tiles)
    Inputs/usgs_hydrography/hydr48m010g.gdb               (water bodies)
    Inputs/usgs_major_dams/dams00x020.shp                 (major dams, with its .dbf, .shx, .prj)
    Inputs/city_populations/1790-2010_MASTER.csv          (town populations)
    Inputs/gnis/DomesticNames_<state>.txt                 (place names and points, one file per state)
    Inputs/nhgis/nhgis_ts_nominal_county.csv              (census population of every county)

All of them are public and free. A free NHGIS account is needed for the last one.

Elevation (USGS GMTED2010)

Mean elevation at 7.5 arc-seconds, the five tiles that cover the contiguous United States:
10N090W, 10N120W, 30N090W, 30N120W and 30N150W. Each is a file <tile>_20101117_gmted_mea075.tif in the
subfolder W090, W120 or W150 of
https://edcintl.cr.usgs.gov/downloads/sciweb1/shared/topo/downloads/GMTED/Global_tiles_GMTED/075darcsec/mea/

Water bodies (National Atlas)

1:1,000,000-Scale Hydrographic Geodatabase of the United States, Conterminous United States
(hydr48m010g, 2014). Unpack
https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Small-scale/data/Hydrography/hydr48m010g.gdb_nt00893.tar.gz

Major dams (National Atlas)

Major Dams of the United States (dams00x020, 2006). Unpack
https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Small-scale/data/Hydrography/dams00x020_nt00010.tar.gz

Town populations (US Historical City Populations)

Census counts of US towns and cities 1790-2010, compiled by Erik Steiner and Jason Heppler, Spatial History
Project, Stanford University. The file 1790-2010_MASTER.csv of
https://github.com/cestastanford/historical-us-city-populations

Place names (USGS GNIS)

Geographic Names Information System, domestic names, one file DomesticNames_<state>.txt per state, from
https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/GeographicNames/DomesticNames/

County population (NHGIS)

Time series table A00 (total population) for nominal counties, 1790-2010, as a csv file, from
https://www.nhgis.org. In the NHGIS data finder, select Time Series Tables, table A00 "Total Population",
geographic level County, and the "Nominal" (not standardized) layout. Save the file as
nhgis_ts_nominal_county.csv.
